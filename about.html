A Consulting gig
that Wouldn't let us sleep

In 2017, our founders were brought in to help a regional healthcare network recover from a breach that had gone undetected for nineteen days. The tools that should have caught it were already installed — they just weren't being watched by anyone who'd notice a 2am anomaly.

That gap, between having security tools and having someone actually paying attention, became the whole reason Warden exists. We're not a software company that added a monitoring team. We started as the monitoring team, and built the rest around it.
