Coverage that doesn't sleep when your team does

We deploy sensors across your endpoints, network and cloud accounts, then staff a rotating team of analysts who watch what they catch — every hour, every day, including the 2am false alarm that turns out to be false.

  • Endpoint, network and cloud telemetry in one view
  • Every alert reviewed by a person before it reaches you
  • Monthly tuning so alert volume drops as coverage improves
  • Direct line to the analyst on shift, not a ticket queue

The team that already knows your network shows up

When something's actively wrong, the last thing you need is to explain your architecture to someone new. Our response team is the same team monitoring you day to day — they're already inside your environment when the call comes in.

  • Retained response with a guaranteed join time, not best-effort
  • Containment, forensic review and recovery under one engagement
  • Runbooks built and tested with your team before you need them
  • A written post-incident report your board can actually read

We test the doors an attacker would actually try

Automated scanners flag what's already documented. Our assessments go further — manual penetration testing and architecture review aimed at the specific paths a real attacker would take through your environment.

  • External and internal penetration testing
  • Cloud configuration and identity review
  • Social engineering testing, with your consent and scope
  • A prioritized findings report, ranked by real exploitability
